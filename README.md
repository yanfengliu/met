# met

A procedural three.js model of the Metropolitan Museum of Art's Fifth Avenue building in New York, outside and inside, that you watch being built.

Live: https://yanfengliu.github.io/met/

The page does not load a finished model. It assembles one from an ordered list of build steps, in the order the real building grew, and you play that build forward or backward, one step at a time or continuously. The finished building is only the last frame.

Every part is generated by code. There are no imported meshes, scans or photo textures, and the page fetches no assets from the network.

## What it shows

The build has nine chapters, from the bare site to the 2014 plaza, and each part appears in the era it was built. Only parts that exist today are modelled. The Wallace Wing and its roof garden, closed in 2025, stand as they did until then, and the step text says the Tang Wing is replacing them.

Inside, it models the major public spaces: the Great Hall and Grand Staircase, the Temple of Dendur gallery, the American Wing's Charles Engelhard Court, the Greek and Roman court, the Arms and Armor court, the Medieval Sculpture Hall, the Lehman Wing court, the Petrie Court, the Astor Court and the skylights of the European Paintings galleries. It does not model individual galleries or artworks, apart from landmark objects such as the Temple of Dendur.

## Run it

You need Node 24 (see `.nvmrc`), npm and a browser with WebGL 2.

```sh
npm ci
npm run dev
```

The dev server listens on `http://127.0.0.1:5310/`. Set `MET_PORT` to a whole number from 1024 to 65535 to use another port. The server fails instead of moving to the next port when its port is taken, so a client never reaches another worktree's server.

The page starts at the empty site and plays forward. Add `?autoplay=0` to stay at the empty site.

`npm run build` writes both pages to `dist/` with relative URLs, so the built site can be served from any path. `npm run preview` serves `dist/` on the same port.

## Controls

### Timeline bar

- Six buttons: Go to start, Step back, Play backward, Play or Pause, Step forward, Go to end.
- Four speeds: 0.5×, 1×, 2× and 4×. A step of normal weight takes 3 seconds at 1×.
- The scrubber covers the whole build, and thin marks on it show where each chapter starts. Drag it to any point.
- The label reads `Step k / N · year · chapter — title`, with the step's detail under it. Before the first step it reads `Empty site`.
- Step forward and Step back move to the next or previous whole step, and pressing again while moving retargets. Play forward at the end starts again from the start, and Play backward at the start starts from the end.

### Keyboard

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| Space | Play or pause |
| Shift+Space | Play backward, or pause |
| Right arrow | Step forward |
| Left arrow | Step back |
| Home | Go to start |
| End | Go to end |
| Escape | Close an open menu |

A focused button keeps Space and Enter for itself, and a focused slider other than the scrubber keeps its arrow keys, Home and End.

### Chapters menu

It lists the nine chapters with their years. Choosing one jumps to the start of its first step, and the chapter you are in is marked.

### View menu

- **Exterior** and **Interior**: camera presets. The ids for `view=` are in the tables below. The page opens on `aerial-ne`.
- **Follow the build**: while the build plays, the camera frames what each step adds. It turns off as soon as you drag or zoom the camera yourself (a press without movement leaves it on), and the toggle turns it back on. It starts on unless the URL sets `shot=1`, a `view=` that names a preset, or `cam=`; a `view=` that names no preset is ignored.
- **Section cut**: Cut switches a horizontal cut on or off, and the slider sets its height in metres, in 0.1 m steps up to just above the model's top. Moving the slider switches the cut on. Only the building is cut: everything of it above the cut is removed, shadows included, and cut walls and slabs are drawn solid in one flat colour, as in an architect's section, while everything the site builds as landscape (trees, ground, streets, paths, rocks, walls, fences, lamps, benches, fountains and the plaza's furniture) stays whole. Use it, or an interior preset, to see inside.

### Camera

Drag to orbit, right-drag to pan, and use the wheel or a pinch to zoom toward the pointer. The camera stays above the ground.

## URL parameters

`src/viewer/query.ts` is the only reader of the page URL. A malformed or unknown value is ignored, with a console warning that names the parameter and what would be accepted.

| Parameter | Effect |
|---|---|
| `t=<number>` | Start at this position in the build, from 0 to N. Values outside the range are clamped. |
| `step=<id or 1-based index>` | Start at the end of that step. It wins over `t`. |
| `view=<preset id>` | Open on that camera preset. |
| `cam=px,py,pz,tx,ty,tz` | Open with this camera position and target, in metres. |
| `cut=<metres>` | Switch the section cut on at this height. |
| `solo=<chapter id>` | Build only that chapter, plus `site`. |
| `autoplay=0` or `autoplay=1` | Do not start playing on load, or play even when the URL sets a position. |
| `noui=1` | Hide the page chrome. |
| `shot=1` | Capture mode: no autoplay, no follow camera, no animation. `window.__shotReady` becomes true once at least 5 frames have rendered at the requested state. |

Coordinates are in the world frame: metres, +Y up, +X up Fifth Avenue (Manhattan north), +Z toward Fifth Avenue. The origin is at sidewalk grade on the central entrance axis, in the plane of the Hunt facade's main wall face.

`window.__met` exposes read-only facts for tests and capture scripts: step ids, chapters, presets, the current position, camera and cut, budget statistics and build timings. Tests drive the page through its real controls and read state from it.

## Parts gallery

`http://127.0.0.1:5310/parts.html` shows every part of the parametric library in `src/model/lib/` on a labelled grid: arches, balustrades, domes and vaults, Egyptian parts, fittings, glazing, landscape, mouldings, orders (columns, pilasters, capitals), stairs, walls and windows.

| Parameter | Effect |
|---|---|
| `part=<id>` | Show one exhibit alone and frame it, for example `part=column-corinthian`. |
| `view=<name>` | The angle: `three-quarter` (default), `front`, `side`, `back`, `rear-quarter`, `top`, `low` or `up`. |
| `closeup=<name>` | Frame one of the exhibit's named close-up boxes. |
| `shot=1` | Capture mode, as on the main page. |
| `noui=1` | Hide the labels. |

## Checks

`npm run verify` is the pre-commit gate: unit tests, type check, lint, build and the Playwright end-to-end suite on the installed Chrome. What each step covers, the capture commands (`npm run shots`, `npm run sweep`) and the dependency audits are in [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md), under Gates.

## Where things are

- `src/build/`: the build engine: step types, batching into instanced meshes, timeline evaluation, the player, the chapter list and the composed sequence.
- `src/model/`: the model. `frame.ts` holds the world-frame constants, each with its source, `palette.ts` the shared materials, `lib/` the parametric parts, and `site`, `core`, `hunt`, `mmw`, `roche-north` and `roche-south` the six modelling areas.
- `src/viewer/`: renderer, camera, presets, section cut and URL parsing. `src/ui/`: the timeline bar and menus. `src/parts/`: the gallery page.
- `scripts/`: capture tooling. `tests/e2e/`: Playwright tests.
- `docs/design/spec.md`: the contract for the world frame, the build-step API, the page and the budgets.
- `docs/research/`: the research behind the model. `exterior.md` has the footprint, the blocks and the dimensions, `interior.md` the interior spaces, `history.md` the chronology and the chapter list, and `exterior-images.md` and `interior-images.md` the reference photographs.
- `docs/work/0_met-model/`: the plan and its review rounds. `docs/devlog/`: the history. `docs/policies/local-rules.md`: the rules that bind only this repository.

## Reference photographs

The model is checked against photographs of the real building, but the photographs are not in this repository and are never committed. `docs/research/exterior-images.md` and `docs/research/interior-images.md` list each one with its URL, author, licence, what it shows and where the camera stood. Local copies, renders and captures live only under ignored paths: `.shots/`, `output/`, `tmp/` and `research/cache/`.
