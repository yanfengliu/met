# The Met Fifth Avenue — building history and chapter proposal

A dated chronology of the parts of the building that exist today, who built them, and which massing block in `exterior.md` each one is, ending in a proposed chapter list for the build timeline. Compiled 2026-10-11 by the `research-exterior` worker and a history subagent.

Tags: **(r)** read in the full source; **(s)** from search-result snippets only; **inf.** inference by this research.

## Conflicts

- North wing (1894) architect: Weston with Tuckerman (L77) or Tuckerman alone (NHL).
- Hunt hired 1885 (NHL, probably a typo) or 1895 (L77, after the act of 16 April 1895).
- 1880 building: ground broken 1874 and built from 1877 (L77), or designed and begun 1874 (NHL).
- MMW dates: build spans 1906–11, 1910–13, 1914–17, 1914–26 (L77); completions 1911, 1913, 1916, 1926 (L67); openings 1910 (Morgan Wing), 1911, 1913, 1916, 1926 (NHL); E 1907 and J 1917 (BL, s); "finished in 1910" (WM).
- Master plan: commissioned 1967 (L77, HDC, AR), dated 1970 (AR), approved and published 1971 (NHL, s).
- Kravis Wing 1990 (BR) or 1991 (AR). Assay Office facade 1823 (L67) or 1822 (s). Post's middle initial B (L77) or C (NHL).
- Tang Wing: 125,000 (AR) or 126,000 sq ft (HY); opening 2029 (AR) or 2030 (HY).
- Fifth Avenue front: "900-foot facade" (NHL) versus 304.7 m (1,000 ft) measured in OSM (exterior.md W1); the plaza is quoted at 1,021 ft (PZ, s). Overall length "almost a quarter mile (400 m)" (W5, WM).
- Floor area: "more than 2 million square feet" (W5, WM); 2.1 million sq ft in 21 attached structures (AR, 2024); in 1967 "the equivalent of four city blocks and seventeen and a half acres of floor space" (L67). No published footprint area was found; the measured footprint is 46,470 m² (exterior.md B00).
- Wing letters (A–D for 1880–1902, E–K for MMW) come from snippets and Heckscher's convention; they were not confirmed in a source read in full.

## Decisions for the coordinator

- Southwest corner: the Tang Wing broke ground on 15 Sep 2026 inside the Wallace Wing's envelope, so "present day" there is a construction site. Proposal: model the Wallace Wing and Roof Garden as they stood until October 2025, with a step detail saying they are being replaced; the alternative is to stop the south chapter at the Rockefeller and Kravis wings and leave the corner empty.
- Rockefeller glass wall: built 1982, rebuilt 2021–25 to the same look. Proposal: build it in the 1982 step and mention the 2025 rebuild in the step detail.
- European Paintings skylights: the openings date from MMW (1907) and the glazing from 2023. Proposal: build the skylights with their roof in the core chapter and note the 2023 renewal in a late step that adds nothing visible, or skip the note.
- The 1924 American Wing and the mid-century infill (auditorium 1954, library 1965) are enclosed today; they appear only as interior placeholders inside later blocks.

## Proposed chapters

Each step list is in constructional order: ground and footprint, structure and walls, openings and ornament, interior placeholder, roof. Block ids refer to `exterior.md` section 2.

### 1. `site` — Site — before 1872 (present-day ground)

The ground the museum stands on: the park's terrain, Fifth Avenue and its sidewalks.

1. `site-terrain` Park ground: a terrain surface from the DEM samples, +1 to +9 m on the park side, sloping to grade at Fifth Avenue.
2. `site-rock` Schist outcrops north-west of the building.
3. `site-fifth-avenue` Fifth Avenue carriageway, 14 m curb to curb, curbs and gutters.
4. `site-east-sidewalk` East sidewalk and the building line opposite (placeholder blocks).
5. `site-park-paths` East Drive and the park paths around the site.
6. `site-transverse-cuts` The service drive and garage ramp cuts on the north and south sides.
7. `site-trees-park` Park trees around the building (sparse, generic).
8. `site-pad` Building pad: the footprint outline marked on the ground.

### 2. `vaux-mould-1880` — The first building — 1874–1880

Vaux and Mould's brick Gothic building in the park, the core everything else wraps.

1. `vaux-foundations` Foundations and basement of core-1880-a.
2. `vaux-walls` Red-brick walls with granite bands, the long side walls.
3. `vaux-west-front` West front with its pointed-arch entrance (survives inside the Lehman Wing).
4. `vaux-openings` Pointed-arch windows and granite trim.
5. `vaux-iron-frame` Radford's iron frame and the restored iron staircases (interior placeholder).
6. `vaux-hall` Central hall (today the Medieval Sculpture Hall), interior placeholder.
7. `vaux-roof` Pitched roofs with the skylit upper gallery.

### 3. `weston-tuckerman-1894` — The first wings — 1884–1894

Weston's south wing and Tuckerman's north wing extend the brick building in the same style.

1. `wing-b-foundations` Foundations of core-1888-b.
2. `wing-b-walls` Brick and stone walls of the south wing.
3. `wing-b-south-facade` Weston's classical south facade (survives in the Petrie Court).
4. `wing-c-foundations` Foundations of core-1894-c.
5. `wing-c-walls` Brick and stone walls of the north wing.
6. `wings-openings` Windows and the south entrance.
7. `wings-galleries` Upper picture galleries, interior placeholder.
8. `wings-roofs` Slate roofs and gallery skylights.

### 4. `hunt-1902` — Hunt's Fifth Avenue front — 1895–1902

The Beaux-Arts pavilion, Great Hall and Grand Staircase that turned the museum to face Fifth Avenue.

1. `hunt-foundations` Excavation and foundations of hunt-d.
2. `hunt-podium` Podium and landing level of the pavilion.
3. `hunt-walls` Limestone walls of the pavilion and the flanking extensions.
4. `hunt-pedestals` Four pedestals of the column pairs.
5. `hunt-columns` Eight Corinthian columns and their wall pilasters.
6. `hunt-arches` Three great arches with doorway screens and lunette windows.
7. `hunt-entablature` Entablature with breaks over the column pairs.
8. `hunt-attic` Attic wall and the ornamental cresting.
9. `hunt-blocks` Four uncarved stone blocks over the column pairs.
10. `hunt-sculpture` Six medallions, keystone masks, caryatids on the extensions.
11. `hunt-great-hall` Great Hall with its three domes, interior placeholder.
12. `hunt-grand-staircase` Grand Staircase, interior placeholder.
13. `hunt-roof` Roof with the hall skylight and end skylights.

### 5. `mckim-mead-white-1926` — The Fifth Avenue wings — 1904–1926

McKim, Mead & White complete the thousand-foot front and fill the north side behind it.

1. `mmw-north-foundations` Foundations of mmw-north-e and mmw-north-h.
2. `mmw-north-walls` North link and north end pavilion walls.
3. `mmw-north-openings` Windows, pilasters and cornice of the north wings.
4. `mmw-morgan-wing` Rear north wings (mmw-north-f), year 1910.
5. `mmw-south-foundations` Foundations of mmw-south-j and mmw-south-k.
6. `mmw-south-walls` South link and south end pavilion walls.
7. `mmw-south-openings` Windows, pilasters and cornice of the south wings.
8. `mmw-courts` Light wells and the Roman court, interior placeholder.
9. `mmw-american-wing-1924` Atterbury's American Wing core and the bank facade, year 1924, interior placeholder.
10. `mmw-roofs` Roofs and skylights of the MMW wings.

### 6. `roche-steps-lehman-1975` — The master plan begins — 1967–1975

Roche Dinkeloo rebuild the front steps and add the Lehman Wing on the axis.

1. `steps-landing` The landing across the pavilion front, year 1970.
2. `steps-flight` The broad flight of about 22 steps, year 1970.
3. `steps-railings` Handrails and side railings, year 1970.
4. `lehman-foundations` Lehman Wing foundations against the 1880 west front.
5. `lehman-walls` Limestone walls with the cut western corners.
6. `lehman-court` Skylit court and period rooms, interior placeholder.
7. `lehman-pyramid` Glass pyramid roof.

### 7. `roche-north-1980` — North on the park — 1975–1980

The Sackler Wing for the Temple of Dendur and the new American Wing with its glass court.

1. `sackler-foundations` Sackler Wing foundations and terrace.
2. `sackler-walls` Limestone side walls.
3. `sackler-glass-wall` Sloped glass wall facing the park.
4. `sackler-temple` Temple of Dendur and its pool, interior landmark.
5. `sackler-roof` Glass roof over the temple gallery.
6. `american-foundations` American Wing foundations.
7. `american-walls` Limestone walls around the 1924 core.
8. `american-glass-wall` Engelhard Court glass wall facing west.
9. `american-court` Charles Engelhard Court with the bank facade, interior placeholder.
10. `american-roof` Ribbed glass roofs.

### 8. `roche-south-1990` — South on the park — 1979–1991

The Rockefeller, Wallace and Kravis wings close the south side, with the roof garden on top.

1. `rockefeller-foundations` Rockefeller Wing foundations.
2. `rockefeller-walls` Limestone walls.
3. `rockefeller-glass-wall` Sloping south glass wall, 61 m long and 18 m high (rebuilt 2025 to the same look).
4. `rockefeller-roof` Ridge skylights over the floor 2 galleries.
5. `wallace-structure` Wallace Wing, four floors.
6. `wallace-facades` Limestone and glass facades on the park.
7. `roof-garden` Cantor Roof Garden terrace, planters and pergola, year 1987.
8. `kravis-structure` Kravis Wing on the west face.
9. `petrie-court` Petrie Court glass enclosure against the 1888 facade.
10. `kravis-roof` Roofs and the court's glass roof.

### 9. `koch-plaza-2014` — The plaza and renewals — 2007–2025

The OLIN plaza replaces the 1970 plaza; late renewals finish the present-day building.

1. `roman-court` Leon Levy and Shelby White Court, year 2007, interior placeholder.
2. `plaza-paving` Granite paving from the facade to the curb, year 2014.
3. `plaza-fountains` Two square granite fountains with ring jets, year 2014.
4. `plaza-groves` Four planted groves beside the fountains, year 2014.
5. `plaza-allees` Four rows of 13 trees in front of the end pavilions, year 2014.
6. `plaza-furniture` Benches, lights and the red umbrella tables, year 2014.
7. `skylights-2023` European Paintings skylight glazing renewed, year 2023.
