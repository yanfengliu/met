## Reports

### Codex (`gpt-6-astra`, xhigh)

The approach is viable, but the door integration and several acceptance contracts need revision before implementation. Reviewed `cfd128a`; findings below concern the design, not implemented defects.

1. **F1 — High — Hiding leaves does not yet preserve determinism.** [Plan:31](C:/Users/38909/Documents/github/met/docs/work/2_doors-and-collection/plan.md:31). The timeline owns matrices, colours, counts and `visible`; changing any to suppress a leaf violates its contract. An in-memory probe confirmed that `evaluate(N)` leaves an externally hidden mesh hidden because unchanged steps are skipped. Batching also prevents hiding an arbitrary instance independently. Specify door-only batches and a presentation filter that leaves timeline-owned fields untouched. Document door state as an explicit viewer input, and add snapshot comparisons with the door layer attached across interactions and random timeline histories. The existing timeline-only tests cannot prove this isolation.

2. **F2 — High — Moving collision instances requires more than updating their matrices.** [Plan:31](C:/Users/38909/Documents/github/met/docs/work/2_doors-and-collection/plan.md:31). `CollisionWorld.build()` copies matrices, inverses, bounds and grid membership; subsequent mesh changes do not move collision ([collision.ts:140](C:/Users/38909/Documents/github/met/src/walk/collision.ts:140)). Specify a small dynamic collision world or an update API that maintains all these structures. Check the swept motion against the capsule during opening **and** closing: opening away from the walker initially does not protect someone walking into the swing afterward. Advance doors consistently with physics substeps, and test low frame rates, stalled frames, reversal and obstruction. Define how dynamic instance identities interact with `settle()`.

3. **F3 — High — The stand-ins are absent from the cap, pick and redraw contracts.** [Plan:31](C:/Users/38909/Documents/github/met/docs/work/2_doors-and-collection/plan.md:31). Both picker and cap sources are captured from timeline meshes at boot ([main.ts:88](C:/Users/38909/Documents/github/met/src/main.ts:88)). Replacing their appearance alone leaves picking at the closed doorway and either missing or obsolete section caps. Specify registration of replacement meshes, exclusion of originals, current bounds covering the swing, clipping materials, and render/shadow invalidation throughout motion. Test an open and partly open door under a cut, then zoom and interact through the vacated opening and against the moved leaf.

4. **F4 — Medium — Door lifecycle and screenshot precedence are ambiguous.** [Plan:31](C:/Users/38909/Documents/github/met/docs/work/2_doors-and-collection/plan.md:31). “Takes a door’s step away” must distinguish becoming incomplete from disappearing entirely. Otherwise an open proxy can survive while its canonical leaf starts reversing. Define ownership at `f < 1` and `f = 1`, reset timing, default angle/direction without a walker, and precedence among `doors=`, locked doors and anti-entrapment. In particular, `doors=open` must not unlock the doors guarding drops. Require deterministic `shot=1` initialization before walk placement and readiness, plus stable captures afterward. Test scrubbing across completion while open, rebuilding around the walker, and both query values.

5. **F5 — Medium — The shared look-target contract cannot yet support all consumers.** [Plan:29–34](C:/Users/38909/Documents/github/met/docs/work/2_doors-and-collection/plan.md:29). `ModelPicker.distance()` returns no object identity and explicitly stops at glass ([pick.ts:39](C:/Users/38909/Documents/github/met/src/viewer/pick.ts:39)); the proposed vitrines would therefore conceal their contents from label targeting. Agree a ray-query result containing stable object/door identity, instance mapping and distance, usable by both the walking eye and an orbit pointer click. Define vitrine ownership or transparent-case traversal separately from zoom’s glass-stopping rule, while retaining wall occlusion. Also specify action-time target refresh and route walk-mode E before the keyboard handler’s existing held-movement early return.

6. **F6 — Medium — “Checked offline” does not establish catalogue provenance or safe regeneration.** [Plan:30,42](C:/Users/38909/Documents/github/met/docs/work/2_doors-and-collection/plan.md:30). An ignored cache and a test of the generated file’s shape cannot establish that its contents came from the API. Retain compact source fixtures/provenance, fetch dates, object IDs, source/image hashes and sampler version; test normalization against independent expected values. Specify all-or-nothing output replacement so a block, malformed response or gallery mismatch preserves the previous catalogue. The runtime check should reject all external asset requests, rather than only `metmuseum.org` hosts, and run without the research cache. API availability is expressly not guaranteed. [Met terms](https://www.metmuseum.org/policies/terms-and-conditions)

7. **F7 — Medium — “Real size” and sampled colour need explicit missing-data rules.** [Plan:30,42](C:/Users/38909/Documents/github/met/docs/work/2_doors-and-collection/plan.md:30). Measurements can describe multiple elements—object, frame, pedestal—and include weights as well as spatial dimensions. Height-based ratios produce estimates, not fully measured dimensions. Define element selection, axis mapping, centimetre conversion, validation and explicit overrides; retain which dimensions were estimated. Likewise, metadata being CC0 does not make every object image eligible: require public-domain image evidence before sampling and choose a documented fallback or exclude image-less picks. Mark inferred forms, dimensions and colours as derived placeholders in the catalogue/labels. [API fields](https://metmuseum.github.io/), [image policy](https://www.metmuseum.org/policies/image-resources), [dataset usage guidance](https://github.com/metmuseum/openaccess#additional-usage-guidelines)

8. **F8 — Medium — Freeze shared interfaces before parallel implementation.** [Plan:27–34,48](C:/Users/38909/Documents/github/met/docs/work/2_doors-and-collection/plan.md:27). M3a’s harvesting can run alongside M1, but placement depends on M1’s final routes and geometry. M3b depends on **both M3a and M2**. Assign integration ownership for `main.ts`, `pick.ts`, `walk-mode.ts`, `facts.ts`, UI and spec changes; agree piece-to-object metadata before doors and collection use it. Coordinate insertion points in area arrays and modifications around `hunt/stair.ts` and `roche-north/sackler.ts`. Also resolve the literal “timeline.test.ts unchanged” requirement: its recording Kit implements only `add` and `light` ([timeline.test.ts:37](C:/Users/38909/Documents/github/met/src/build/timeline.test.ts:37)), so a required new Kit method needs an adapter update while preserving every assertion. Splitting door roles also requires updating the composite-role gate.

9. **F9 — Medium — Existing budget gates miss the new expensive states.** [Plan:41–44](C:/Users/38909/Documents/github/met/docs/work/2_doors-and-collection/plan.md:41). The measured baseline is **696 meshes and 1,541,544 triangles**, leaving 204 meshes. Allocate that headroom across placeholders and door representations; define accounting for viewer-side replacement draws because `Timeline.stats()` excludes them. The current walking performance test walks near the plaza without operating doors. A 0.7-second animation can also disappear inside a 120-frame median. Measure sustained door cycles and look-target queries in populated interiors, including cold preparation, with work/frame/GPU timing. Retain the 2-second collision-build and 4-second navigation-to-ready gates.

10. **F10 — Medium — Route clearance does not establish usable placement.** [Plan:30,40–43](C:/Users/38909/Documents/github/met/docs/work/2_doors-and-collection/plan.md:30). Keeping an object’s position 1 m from R1–R4 does not keep its full sculpture, plinth or vitrine clear; it also omits the new gallery-600 route and Weston passage. Check full placed bounds against walking corridors, door swing volumes and neighbouring geometry. After all milestones are integrated, walk the affected routes both ways, traverse Weston, exercise pool escape and dais access, and approach representative displays to trigger labels. Opening a door visually is insufficient evidence that its passage works.

11. **F11 — Medium — The first-person acceptance criteria leave important behaviour undefined.** [Plan:38–39](C:/Users/38909/Documents/github/met/docs/work/2_doors-and-collection/plan.md:38). “Hides what it does not need” could remove the only usable timeline controls during walking, contrary to the current spec. Name retained controls, how hidden controls are revealed, how walking is exited, and how label links are used after pointer lock. Test these flows, not just viewport layout. For stairs, distinguish monotonic ascent from smooth descent and bound visual jumps and eye-height error at several frame rates; simply removing easing could satisfy “never drops” while still feeling poor.

Sound areas: the amended non-goals explicitly permit procedural placeholders and sampled colours. The CC0 metadata/public-domain-image distinction is correct. One request per second with caching and stopping on 403/429 is sensible and below the documented ceiling. [API documentation](https://metmuseum.github.io/) Inserting steps through composed area arrays and retaining their fit-out chapter/year can satisfy the composition and year gates; artwork dates should remain separate catalogue fields.

No files changed. Probes ran entirely in memory; browser tests and runtime performance gates were not run.
