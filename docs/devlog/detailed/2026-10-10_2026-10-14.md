## The section cut paints sliced park trees red, and only a render showed it

**Timestamp:** after `ad6f16f` (2026-10-13 22:59) and before `b5a5e45` (2026-10-14 10:00), the commit that records it

**Action:** The coordinator's acceptance pass on `ad6f16f` (standard shots, seeded sweep, seed 2026) found one defect, recorded as A1: the section cut slices park trees and paints their crowns red. It is in review round 0 (`docs/work/0_met-model/reviews/0_integration.md`) and assigned to the `repair` worker, who must show it red, then green. No code had changed for it at `b5a5e45`.

**Result:** Found in rendered shots. No test looks at what the cut paints.

**Reasoning:** From reading the code at `b5a5e45`, not yet confirmed by the repair: the section cut redraws every opaque model mesh's back faces in one flat colour (`#7b2f2a`; `src/viewer/section-cut.ts`, `renderCap` in `src/viewer/viewer.ts`) and leaves out only the sky, the ground and transparent meshes (`capExcluded` in `src/main.ts`). That is how cut walls and slabs read as solid. Tree crowns are closed, opaque clumps (`src/model/lib/landscape.ts`), so a sliced crown shows its inside in the cut colour. The header of `section-cut.ts` gives the bound for open surfaces and says closed meshes get a solid cut face; neither it nor the spec says vegetation should be left out.

**Validation:** None yet. The e2e cut test counts changed cells of a 32 × 18 luminance grid when the cut toggles, so it cannot tell a correct cut from one that paints the wrong things.

**Code reviewer comments:** Claude's round-0 report lists the section cut and its back-face pass under "Checked with no finding" ("correct"), and Codex ran no browser. Code review passed what a render showed wrong, which is why fleet rule R16 asks for visual work to be checked visually.
